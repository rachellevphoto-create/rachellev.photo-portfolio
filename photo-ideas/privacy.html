Photo Ideas – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Photo Ideas is a small email service run by Rachel Lev Photography for photography students. You send an email with tag words (for example "sunset, beach"), and the service replies with inspiration photos that have those tags.

What we read

  • Only emails sent to send.me.photo.ideas@gmail.com.
  • From each email we use: the sender address, the subject, and the first line of the text.
  • We do not open attachments or links.

What we keep

  • A short log line per request: sender address, time, and the tags asked for.
  • The request email and our reply are moved to the trash right after the reply, and Gmail deletes them after 30 days.
  • We do not sell or share any data. We do not use it for ads or AI training.

Google user data

The service uses the Gmail API only for its own mailbox (send.me.photo.ideas@gmail.com), to read requests, send replies, and move handled mail to the trash. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can use it

Only approved email addresses get a reply. Other emails are ignored.

Contact

send.me.photo.ideas@gmail.com
